#### Who am I and why I become a programmer? 

To introduce, I'd better start with why I become a software engineer at my 34 in a country where many programmers lost jobs at the age of 35. There was a big non-IT company in my hometown, a small county, recruiting Java programmers. I wanted to stay home since it was absolutely difficult for me to settle in a metropolis. Fortunately, I learned all the stacks for a Java developer by myself and got my first job as a programmer in a city close to my home even though I was not employed by the big company. 

Besides, I firmly believe that a craftsman can't be unemployed if he or she were competent. Not all professionals can teach themselves in such a welcoming and vibrant online community where a substantial number of prominent software engineers or hackers contribute to help someone like to to study. Furthermore, most of the tutorials are free, not to mention to considerable number of open-source software for me to learn.
