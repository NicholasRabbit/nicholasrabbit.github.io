#### Who am I? 

I am a software engineer, like my first tutor said. He told me I was a engineer in his first free online course even before writing the first line of code. I had been working in a metropolis for years and wanted to find a job to stay with my family. Then I kept on learning and become a software developer. 

#### Why do I build this website? 

As a middle-aged man, I want to write and read my writings when I am older, which have a sentimental value for me. On the other hand, I would like to share my thoughts and I am very appreciate my readers' feedback.
